Do not upload confidential identity documents unless the tool clearly states how processing occurs. Delete downloaded outputs and clear browser storage on shared computers.

Cookies and local storage

We and our service providers may use cookies or local storage for settings, analytics, advertising, saved drafts and recent calculations. Browser controls can remove this data.

Your choices
